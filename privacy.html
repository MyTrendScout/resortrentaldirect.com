Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Resort Rental Direct ("we," "us," or "our") helps travelers rent resort stays directly from owners. This policy explains what information we collect on resortrentaldirect.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you request a quote, we collect what you enter in the form:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your travel details, such as destination, dates and number of guests

Like most websites, we also receive basic technical information automatically, such as your browser type, device, pages visited and the site that referred you.

How we use your information

  • To find available resort rentals and send you a quote
  • To contact you by email, phone or text about your request
  • To understand which pages and ads bring visitors, so we can improve the site

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising and analytics

We use tools from Google (Google Ads), Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok to measure how our ads perform. These services may set cookies or similar technologies to recognize your browser and record actions such as a completed quote request. You can manage ad personalization in your Google ad settings and Facebook ad preferences, or block cookies in your browser settings.

Who we share it with

We share your information only as needed to handle your request:

  • Resort owners and booking partners, so they can confirm availability and pricing for your stay
  • Service providers that run our website, form delivery and email on our behalf
  • Authorities, when required by law

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, we may text you about your quote. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests only as long as needed to help with your trip and meet our business and legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email us at the address below and we will respond promptly.

Children

This site is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
