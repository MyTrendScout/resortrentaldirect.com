Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

MyPaceZone ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use MyPaceZone:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and a hashed (encrypted) version of your password.
  • Profile data: Age, resting heart rate, fitness level, race date, and training goals you provide during setup.
  • Workout data: Workouts you log manually, including distance, duration, heart rate, and notes.
  • Strava data: If you connect your Strava account, we access your activity history (runs, rides, swims, and other workouts) via the Strava API. We store your Strava access tokens securely to enable syncing.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide the MyPaceZone service:

  • To calculate personalized heart rate zones based on your profile
  • To build and display your marathon training plan
  • To track your workouts and predict race finish times
  • To sync activities from Strava into your training log
  • To send you access approval and account-related communications

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not share your data with third parties except as described in this policy.

3. Strava Integration

MyPaceZone uses the Strava API to import your workout activities. When you connect Strava:

  • We request read-only access to your activities (activity:read_all scope)
  • Your Strava access and refresh tokens are stored securely in our database
  • We only read activity data — we never write to or modify your Strava account
  • You can disconnect Strava at any time from within the app, which removes your tokens from our system

MyPaceZone's use of Strava data is governed by the Strava API Agreement.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore, a secure cloud database. Passwords are never stored in plain text — we store only a SHA-256 hash of your password. Access to your data is restricted to your account only.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you would like your data deleted, contact us at the email below and we will remove it within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Disconnect any third-party integrations (such as Strava) at any time

7. Medical Disclaimer

MyPaceZone is a training tool, not a medical device or medical service. Heart rate zone calculations and training recommendations are for informational purposes only and do not constitute medical advice. Consult your doctor before beginning any training program, especially if you have a pre-existing health condition.

8. Children's Privacy

MyPaceZone is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify active users of any material changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
