Common Questions

Answers, before you have to ask.

If your question isn't here, call or email — a specialist will reply same day on weekdays.

How does owner-direct timeshare rental actually work?
Timeshare owners often have weeks they can't use in a given year — schedule conflicts, kids' calendars, life. Rather than letting that week go to waste, they rent it. We connect them with travelers who want that resort stay. The owner gets paid for unused inventory, you get a fully furnished resort villa or condo at well below retail, and our specialists handle every detail of the booking and confirmation.
Is this legitimate? Who is The Timeshare Brokers, LLC?
Resort Rental Direct is the consumer-facing brand of The Timeshare Brokers, LLC — an Orlando-based vacation rental specialist established in 2015. Eleven-plus years of operating history, hundreds of completed bookings across major branded properties. You can verify the parent company at thetimesharebrokers.com.
Why are owner-direct prices so much lower than booking the resort directly?
You're booking an unused timeshare week from the original owner — not from the resort's nightly inventory. Owners price below what the resort would charge transient guests because their goal is to recover their annual maintenance fees, not turn a profit. Savings typically run 30 to 60 percent off direct booking, sometimes more.
Will I get the same resort amenities as a regular guest?
Yes. You check in like any other guest and have full access to pools, restaurants, concierge, valet, and on-property amenities. The accommodations themselves are typically larger than hotel rooms — full kitchens, separate bedrooms, washer/dryer in many units. That's the point of the resort timeshare format.
What if my dates change or the booking falls through?
Confirmed bookings are guaranteed. We work directly with verified owner inventory and confirm reservations in writing before you pay. If a rare issue arises, our specialists handle the resolution directly — that's why we operate on a personal-service model rather than as a faceless platform.
How do I pay? Is my payment protected?
Payment is processed through The Timeshare Brokers, LLC after your reservation is confirmed in writing. We accept major credit cards. The booking confirmation comes directly from the resort itself, in your name, before final payment is collected.
What happens after I submit a quote request?
On weekdays, expect a response from a real specialist within four hours — often the same day. We pull matching inventory, send you 2-3 options with full resort details and pricing, and answer any questions before you commit. No high-pressure sales calls, ever.
Which resort brands and locations do you cover?
Marriott Vacation Club, Hilton Grand Vacations, Wyndham, Hyatt Residence Club, Disney Vacation Club, Westin, Bluegreen, Diamond Resorts, Westgate, and others. Top destinations include Orlando, Hawaii, Aruba, the Caribbean, Las Vegas, ski markets like Tahoe and Vail, plus international properties. If you have a specific resort in mind, just ask.
